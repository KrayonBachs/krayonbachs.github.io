About Me

The Person Behind the Schedule

I'm Alex Krabach — a producer and studio lead with a track record of shipping titles across multiple studios. I founded Quantum Shrimp Studio in 2023 and have led cross-discipline teams of up to 16 people from pre-production through release.

My job is to protect the team's time, translate between disciplines, and make sure what we set out to build is what actually ships. I hold a Bachelor's in Digital Gaming and Interactive Media from LWIT and have professional experience at Apple as a Technical Expert — supporting and training team members under real-world pressure.

When I'm not producing, I'm writing — I've self-published four novels available through Amazon in print, audiobook, and ebook.
